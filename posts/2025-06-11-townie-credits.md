Today, we are introducing a new credits payment system for
[Townie](https://townie.val.run/), our AI coding assistant. Townie is now
pay-per-use. Here's the upside:

1. You can use Townie as much as you want – no limits
2. Townie is now priced independently from
   [Val Town Pro](http://val.town/pricing), which makes it cheaper for casual
   Townie users
3. Townie becomes sustainable
4. We at Val Town get a clearer signal about how much to invest in making Townie
   better

## How credits work

We charge a 50% markup on top of raw LLM costs. If you use $10 in Townie
credits, Anthropic will get $6.66 and we'll get $3.33. We think this is fair,
sustainable, and transparent. We don't want to be in the business of having
murky limits, obfuscated credits, or unsustainable margins.

When you pay for Townie, you're telling us in the clearest way possible to
continue to invest in Townie. If you don't think Townie is worth the cost, there
are
[many wonderful AI coding alternatives that you can use with the new
Val Town CLI](https://docs.val.town/quickstarts/prompting/#local-development).
We want you to choose Townie on its merits, not because it's subsidized.

## Start coding with Townie

We think Townie is one of the best coding assistants out there. It uses agentic
tool-calling, powered by Claude 4 Sonnet, to read and write your code. It can
even make test HTTP calls and inspect logs & traces to see how things are
working, and keep iterating from there. Paired with Val Town's instant (100ms)
full-stack deploys, Townie is one of the fastest ways to create a deployed app.
