Dear investors and friends,

Val Town is the deployment platform for go-to-market engineering. Our mission is
spreading the joy of programming.

## June 2026

ARR dropped 1%. We grew 16% in Pro subscriptions, but lost five Business
customers. Three of those companies shut down, a fourth had a payment issue,
while the fifth was a proper churn.

This month reinforced that the jump from our $25 Pro plan to our $200 Business
plan is too steep. We are redesigning Business pricing around a more gradual,
likely seat-based model that makes it easier for individuals to bring Val Town
into their teams.

We
[shipped a ton of product updates](https://blog.val.town/changelog-2026-07-02),
including making Townie 5x cheaper, and making it easier to transfer
applications from personal accounts to company organizations.

## July 2026

Our goal for July remains growing MRR 20%.

We are sharpening our focus around a single initial wedge: **go-to-market
engineering**.

Most technical startups have scripts, bots, dashboards, alerts, and enrichment
workflows that help them understand users and find customers. These tools are
often built by founders or technically inclined go-to-market employees using a
patchwork of scripts, Slack bots, Clay, and traditional cloud infrastructure.

We believe Val Town is the simplest place to build and deploy this software. GTM
engineering is our initial wedge into a broader opportunity: giving everyone a
lightweight platform for the internal tools.
