One of the most common patterns in Val Town is to take data input from some
external source, do something useful with that data, and send output to another
external source.

![in-val-out.svg](/images/51317006-2c2a-43f6-d319-c4c3b08a3b00.svg)

I/O but with a val in the middle: I/V/O. _In, val, out._

## New user Slack alert

The canonical example is a user signup alert in Slack:

1. Receive a webhook when a user signs up (e.g., from
   [Supabase](https://docs.val.town/integrations/supabase/))
2. (Optionally) process or enrich user data in your val (e.g., with
   [Clay](https://blog.val.town/clay))
3. Notify your team on
   [Slack](https://docs.val.town/integrations/slack/send-messages-to-slack/)

![signup-val-slack.svg](/images/d6e6b94d-6d81-4742-48b0-29415fd8c900.svg)

Remix [this val](https://www.val.town/x/stevekrouse/enrich-new-users) to your
liking. Maybe you use in-house auth or another provider like Auth0. Maybe you
want to take the new user's email address and query for existing users from the
same employer. Maybe you'd prefer a Discord notification or an email.

Regardless of the tools you use and the result you want, a val can flexibly
handle it. The in-val-out pattern goes beyond new user alerts, of course. Dozens
of examples are listed on the
[I/V/O docs](https://docs.val.town/guides/in-val-out), but the best examples
will be the vals you create and use. Consider this a Call For Vals.

## In

Input could be customer support [email](https://docs.val.town/std/email) you
receive, stars or new issues on your
[GitHub](https://docs.val.town/integrations/github/receiving-a-github-webhook/)
repo, failed payments from [Stripe](https://docs.val.town/integrations/stripe/),
user signups from [Supabase](https://docs.val.town/integrations/supabase/),
calendar invites, Sentry errors, form submissions—you name it.

## Val

Once you've piped input into a val, you could analyze or classify data with
[OpenAI](https://docs.val.town/std/openai/) or Anthropic, enrich it with browser
automation via [Browserbase](https://docs.val.town/integrations/browserbase/) or
[Kernel](https://docs.val.town/integrations/kernel/), verify phone numbers or
emails with Twilio, process images and video, parse PDFs, or pipe data directly
to an output channel.

## Out

After input and processing, you could notify yourself over email or
[Slack](https://docs.val.town/integrations/slack/send-messages-to-slack/),
create a Linear issue, open a GitHub PR, write to a Notion doc or
[Airtable](https://docs.val.town/integrations/airtable/), update a
[Google Sheet](https://docs.val.town/integrations/google-sheets/), persist data
in [SQLite](https://docs.val.town/std/sqlite/) or
[Supabase](https://docs.val.town/integrations/databases/supabase/), or whatever
else would be useful to you and your team.
