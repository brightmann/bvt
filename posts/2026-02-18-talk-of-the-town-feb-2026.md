We're experimenting with a new _Talk of the Town_ blog series to spotlight neat
vals from the community and other goings-on in Val Town.

## *Val*entine's Day

[mewtru](https://www.val.town/u/mewtru) made an adorable
[valentine](https://www.val.town/x/mewtru/valentine) val—a _val_ val—that over
10k people have remixed! That means to the ~20k people who signed up for Val
Town in the last two weeks, we'll forever be the Valentine's Day company.

![valentine.png](/images/bf4a64bc-45cd-4427-821d-ba2ce4951300.webp)

## Val Town in the Atmosphere

Val Town has made its way into the
[Atmosphere](https://atproto.com/guides/glossary#atmosphere), as in the AT
Protocol ecosystem (or "atproto," for short).
[Dan Abramov](https://bsky.app/profile/danabra.mov) is running code on Val Town
for his new [Inlay](https://bsky.app/profile/did:plc:mdg3w2kpadcyxy33pizokzf3)
"social component" system.

![danabramov-bsky.png](/images/c3228c2b-792f-4441-4529-1023ac39b200.webp)

Dan will be presenting at
[ATmosphereConf 2026](https://ti.to/atmosphereconf/atmosphereconf2026) in March,
and Tom from our team might be there, too—let us know if you're going!

## Val Town in your town

[Paul Chin Jr.](https://www.val.town/u/pchinjr) gave a Val Town demo at the
[NorfolkJS January 2026](https://youtu.be/UNqv3tGKh2I) meetup. We love Paul's
description of Val Town: "a nice place for JavaScript." If you're throwing a
val-centric local meetup or hackathon, let us know! We can order some pizza pies
for you (or a Wegmans charcuterie board, in Paul's case).
