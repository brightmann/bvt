Dear investors and friends,

Val Town is the 100ms deployment platform. Our mission is spreading the joy of
programming.

## May 2026

We grew ARR 3% in May, driven mostly by Pro subscriptions, which in turn is
likely because we made private & unlisted vals no longer accessible on our Free
plan. We also had our first fully-self-serve Business plan subscriber; they
signed up and subscribed without talking to us.

Our May focus was Claude Code + Val Town. It's really fun to use Claude Code and
Codex to make vals!
[In this silly livestream](https://www.youtube.com/live/-eaTnjVs84I?si=cSSMfxwug7Crsedu&t=890),
I spin up 33 parallel Claude Code subagents to deploy 33 demos on Val Town – one
for [every API on Stripe Projects](https://projects.dev/providers/) – in 20
minutes! We built a
[“Claude Plugin”](https://docs.val.town/guides/prompting/plugin/), redesigned
the top of our homepage, and plastered copyable “install our plugin” snippets
pages throughout our site and docs.
